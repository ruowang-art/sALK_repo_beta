Upload Live Label cage-card workbooks

Select one or more .xlsx Live Label cage-card workbooks produced by Möuseley Kräs. Xol-Pots-Xol will cross-compare every cage across all of them and consolidate sparse ones into cages of up to 5 mice, where all of the following match:

  • the same sex,
  • the same strain,
  • the same (normalized) Kras genotype,
  • a date-of-birth window of at most 2 days (males) or 7 days (females).

A mouse that doesn't have a recognized sex, strain, Kras genotype, or usable date of birth is never guessed into a group — it's kept in its original cage grouping in the output instead.

Command-click or Shift-click to select more than one file.
